04 · Research · wireless and embedded

Breaking and fixing keyless entry.

Across my bachelor's and master's theses I built a model of a car's remote locking system at 433 MHz, attacked it with hardware I made, then redesigned it to detect those attacks.

BuildAttackAttackDefend
  1. BuildBachelor's, 2023

    Arduino Car Lock (GitHub, opens in a new tab)

    Two Arduinos acting as a car remote and a door lock, talking over 433 MHz.

  2. AttackOwn hardware

    RF capture and replay device (GitHub, opens in a new tab)

    A Raspberry Pi and RTL-SDR in a waterproof case that records a remote and plays it back.

  3. AttackAbout €5

    433 Jammy (GitHub, opens in a new tab)

    A cheap jammer for the band most European car remotes use.

  4. DefendMaster's, 2025

    Arduino Car Lock Advanced (GitHub, opens in a new tab)

    A rolling counter in every command, so a replayed signal is spotted and refused.

05 · About

Build secure systems. Break insecure ones.

I studied security technologies at Tomas Bata University in Zlín and work on endpoint and infrastructure security, mostly with Microsoft Intune and Microsoft 365.

My interests also cover security monitoring, mobile app analysis and vulnerability research, plus radio: SDR tools, RF signals, wireless systems and embedded devices. Knowing how a system gets attacked is what makes the defence worth trusting.
